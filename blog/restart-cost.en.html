The price of restarting Wegovy, done as a calculation

2026 prices are lower. A restart is still a monthly fixed cost. Put the numbers down before deciding.

2026-10-18

Half of the reason people put off restarting is money, and most never do the sum. There is only a feeling: "that bill again". Numbers make the decision easier.

2026 prices

Prices vary by market and coverage, but the direction in 2026 is down: list prices were cut, direct-to-patient programmes appeared, and oral semaglutide launched below injection prices. In most markets a month at a maintenance dose now costs a fraction of a month at the top dose.

Three restart scenarios

  1. Full dose, like the first time: the highest monthly cost for three to six months. Losing weight again.
  2. Maintenance dose: a fraction of that, open-ended. Keeping the weight you have now.
  3. No drug, routine only: zero. Protein, strength and sleep.

Which one fits is set by regain share from your lowest weight. Under 20%, option three comes first. Over 40%, options one and two are worth discussing with your clinician.

Cost is decided by when

The same 40% at week four and at week twenty are different bills. Caught early, a few months at a maintenance dose may be enough; caught late, it is a full course again. The surest way to lower the cost of restarting is noticing early.
