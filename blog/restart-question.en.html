Thinking about restarting? Three records to bring to your appointment

Whether to restart is decided with your clinician. Three records make that conversation short and precise.

2026-09-13

A few months after stopping, the thought "should I restart?" can come up. The answer depends on your health, other conditions, past side effects, cost and more, and it is worked out with your clinician. Rather than reaching a conclusion alone, prepare the records you will show; they make the conversation short and precise.

1. Weight trend

Starting weight, lowest weight and the latest 7-day average. A record that shows direction rather than a single day.

2. Appetite and food noise

Scores from the last few weeks. They show changes that weight alone does not explain.

3. Dose history and side effects

The date and dose of your last dose, and notes on symptoms that bothered you.

Decisions happen in the clinic

Konoma calculates numbers such as how far you are from your lowest weight, but those numbers summarise your records. They are not a threshold for whether or how to restart a medicine.

In Konoma

The report card at the bottom of Trends turns weight trend, appetite records and dose history into a one-page PDF. Make it before the appointment.

General information. Decisions about lowering, stopping or restarting a medicine belong to you and your clinician. Konoma's numbers and status labels organise your own records; they are not a diagnosis or a prescribing threshold.
