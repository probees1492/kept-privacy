Restart Wegovy? Three numbers to look at first

Decide on evidence, not a feeling: regain share, appetite trend, weeks since the last dose.

2026-09-13

A few months off, a fair amount of weight back, and the same question on loop: should I restart? The question hides two different things. A real need, and plain anxiety. Three numbers separate them.

1. Share of lost weight regained

Measured from your lowest weight, not your starting weight. Started at 78 kg, reached 63, now 69: that is 6 of 15 kg back, or 40%.

Many clinicians use 50% regain as the trigger to intervene again. Kept shows "talk to your doctor" earlier, at 40%. That is the zone where a restart conversation is worth having. Under 20%, routine comes before medication.

2. Four weeks of appetite and food noise

Weight back but appetite scores stable? The cause may be activity or sleep, not the missing drug. Appetite and food noise climbing steadily? The drug's job is vacant. Same 40%, different decision.

3. Weeks since the last dose

Under eight weeks, the body is still finding its new balance and some regain reverses on its own. Past sixteen weeks and still rising, that is a trend.

Restarting has a middle option

Studies from 2025 and 2026 point one way: people who stopped outright regained about two-thirds within a year; people on a reduced dose or longer interval plateaued at less than half of that. With 2026 price cuts and oral semaglutide, half doses and two-week intervals have become realistic costs.

That choice belongs to your clinician. But with the three numbers on one page, the conversation takes five minutes.
