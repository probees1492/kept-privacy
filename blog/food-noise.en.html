Food noise comes back before appetite, and you can measure it

Recurring food thoughts move two to three weeks ahead of the scale.

2026-09-20

The first change people on Wegovy describe is not weight. It is silence. The fridge, the delivery app, the corner shop on the way home stop calling when you are not even hungry. That chatter is food noise.

It is also the first thing that returns when you stop. Most people do not use it as a signal. They notice it in hindsight, after the scale has moved.

The scale is slow; food noise is fast

Weight swings about a kilogram a day with water and salt, so a real change takes two to three weeks to show through the noise. Food noise rises the moment the appetite circuits wake up, typically two to three weeks after the last dose when a quarter of the drug is left.

So the order is: food noise up, portions up, and weeks later the 7-day average follows. Catch step one and step three may never come.

Zero to ten, once a day

The measure is simple: how often did food thoughts show up today, from 0 (not at all) to 10 (constantly). Precision does not matter. What matters is the same person scoring the same way every day.

When the 7-day average climbs by two or more, that is the signal. That week, do one of three things: protein first at every meal, bed thirty minutes earlier, and do not look at the scale. It has not moved yet.
