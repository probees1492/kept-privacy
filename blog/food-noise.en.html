Food noise: what logging it shows you

Score how often food thoughts show up when you are not hungry, from 0 to 10, and see it next to your weight.

2026-09-20

Sometimes food thoughts keep coming back even when you are not hungry. People often call this food noise. It is not a diagnosis, but it is a change many people on or coming off a GLP-1 medicine talk about.

Weight moves every day

Morning weight shifts with the previous day's salt, water, digestion and sleep. So daily weight alone rarely shows changes in appetite or food thoughts.

Zero to ten, once a day

The measure is simple: how often did food thoughts show up today, from 0 (not at all) to 10 (constantly). Precision does not matter. What matters is scoring the same way each day.

After a few weeks the scores form a second line next to weight. With both lines side by side you can describe "how it has been" from records rather than memory.

If the change continues

If the scores stay high or it starts to affect daily life, do not judge it alone; show the records at your appointment.

The appetite and food-noise sliders in the check-in are that record. Trends shows both scores together with weight.
