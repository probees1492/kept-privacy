What oral Wegovy changes, and what it does not

Semaglutide without the needle is here. In maintenance, some things change and the pharmacology does not.

2026-10-11

Oral semaglutide was approved for weight management in 2026, and pills like orforglipron are following. Plenty of headlines; nobody explains what it means for someone two months off the injection.

What changes

First, the threshold. For people who never started or who stopped because of needles, a pill is a different option. Second, price. Pills are cheaper to make and competition is arriving, so they are settling below injections. Third, maintenance. A low daily pill is easier to fine-tune than "half an injection".

What does not change

The pharmacology is the same. Swallowed or injected, it acts on the GLP-1 receptor, and when it stops the appetite circuits return. Half-lives are similar, so the weeks after stopping follow the same curve. Protecting muscle still takes protein and strength work.

So a pill is not "the drug that ends regain". It is "a cheaper, easier way to maintain".

What to do now

A new drug is no reason to stop logging. The opposite: when you eventually discuss "maintain on the pill?" with your clinician, two months of 7-day averages and appetite trends is what gives that conversation evidence.
