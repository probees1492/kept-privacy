Why protein and strength slow regain

A quarter of what you lost was muscle. Less muscle means a lower resting burn and regain on the same food.

2026-10-25

If you lost 10 kg, 2.5 to 4 kg of it was not fat. It was lean mass, mostly muscle. That number repeats across GLP-1 trials. Eating less on the drug meant the body burned some muscle along with the fat.

What less muscle does

A kilogram of muscle burns about 13 kcal a day doing nothing. Lose 4 kg and that is 50 kcal a day, 1,500 a month, gone. It sounds small; it is the pace of 200 g of fat a month. It is one answer to "I eat what I used to, why am I gaining?"

Two things are enough

  • Protein: 1.2 to 1.6 g per kilogram of body weight. At 60 kg, 70 to 100 g a day. Two eggs, a chicken breast, half a block of tofu and a glass of milk get you near 70.
  • Strength: twice a week or more, twenty minutes is plenty. Squats, push-ups, a band. No gym required.

2026 prescribing guidance started listing both as standard companions to GLP-1 therapy. After stopping they matter more, because muscle takes over part of the drug's job.

Ticks, not perfection

You do not need to hit the target exactly every day. Two boxes: "protein today?" and "strength this week?". Five protein days and two sessions is a good week.
