Terms of Use

Service

Kept records your self-reported health information and clinician-agreed plan, and shows descriptive trends and reports. It does not prescribe doses or generate treatment plans. Trend bands and projections are reference rules and assumptions, not clinically validated predictions or diagnoses. Do not use the app to decide when or how much medication to take.

Free access and subscription

Kept offers one 21-day period of Pro access per device without requiring a subscription purchase. That period ends without a charge. Check-ins, dose logs and plan edits remain free. Subscribing during or after this period starts a paid Apple auto-renewing subscription immediately; it is not an Apple introductory subscription trial. The App Store shows the actual localized price and billing period before you confirm.

Renewal and cancellation

Payment is charged to your Apple account at purchase confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless renewal is cancelled at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Manage or cancel through iOS Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. Deleting Kept or erasing records does not cancel a subscription. Apple’s standard End User License Agreement applies: Apple EULA

Your records

You control your entries and backups. You may export JSON, restore a validated backup after confirming replacement, or delete records in the app. Keep a backup if you need your records after replacing or deleting the app. The app cannot delete copies already saved or shared outside it.

サービス

Keptは本人が入力した健康情報と主治医と決めた計画を記録し、推移とレポートを表示します。用量を処方したり、治療計画を作成したりしません。区間や見通しはルールと仮定による参考情報であり、臨床的に検証された予測や診断ではありません。投与時点や量の決定には使用しないでください。
